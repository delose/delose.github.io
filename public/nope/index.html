Safari extension for iPhone and Mac

Pause before you scroll

Nope puts a calm pause page in front of the sites you block in Safari. Leaving takes one tap. Opening a site means a short wait and a few minutes from a daily allowance, so “just five minutes” can't turn into the whole afternoon.

Free. Coming soon to the App Store.

How it works

Block

Pick the sites that eat your time. Blocks cover www., mobile, and every other subdomain.

Pause

Open one, and Nope shows a pause page with the first thing on your own to-do list. Leaving takes one tap.

Decide

Really need it? Wait out a short countdown and open it for a few minutes. When the time is up, the site goes back to the pause page.

Changing your mind works one way

Tightening applies at once: add a site, shrink the allowance, lengthen the wait. Loosening waits out a cooldown, 24 hours by default, so a weak moment can't undo a decision you made with a clear head.

What you get

  • A pause page that shows “Do this instead” from your own to-do list
  • Bypasses that end on their own, from a daily allowance with the reset time shown
  • Streaks for each blocked site, with tiers at 30, 60, 120 and 240 days
  • Quick picks and sites you've seen before, one tap away when you block
  • An optional countdown in the corner of a site while a bypass runs, with End now
Private by design Everything Nope keeps stays on your device. No account, no tracking, no analytics, and no network requests. This page loads nothing from other sites either. Read the privacy policy.

Nope blocks sites in Safari only. Like any extension, it can be turned off in Settings: it is a speed bump for habits, not a parental control. Questions? See Support.
