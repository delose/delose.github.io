What it is

Most apps have you tapping at flashcards. Gaby has you talking. You get a situation — the pharmacy, the NIE appointment, the menú del día — and you say your part out loud. Gaby checks what you actually said, on your device, and shows you the fix when you need one.

What's inside

  • 24 situations covering arrival, errands, eating out, paperwork and social life — the order you actually meet them after moving.
  • Three levels each. Guided shows Spanish and English and you tap. Practice is Spanish only and you speak. Real has no text at all — audio only, which is the one that matches standing in a shop.
  • 100 lessons that build the phrases the situations expect.
  • A 124-piece collection of Spanish paintings and places, unlocked as you work through it and yours to keep.

Why it's different

  • Every correction is written by a person. Nothing in Gaby is generated, so nothing can confidently tell you something wrong.
  • Nothing to download, nothing to sign up for. It makes no network requests at all — it works on a plane, with no account and no roaming.
  • You speak, and it's checked. Speech is transcribed on your device. Matching is generous about accents and word order, because being understood is the real-world win.
  • Pay once. No subscription, ever.

Privacy

Gaby collects nothing. Your progress and preferences stay on your device, your voice is never recorded to a file or sent anywhere, and there is no backend for your data to reach. Read the full Privacy Policy.
